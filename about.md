## Overview

I am a cybersecurity researcher and security professional with over 10 years of experience in offensive security, vulnerability research, and enterprise security assessments. My work focuses on identifying real-world security weaknesses across modern applications, infrastructure, cloud environments, and emerging technologies.

Over the years I have worked across multiple domains of cybersecurity — web application security, network penetration testing, mobile application assessments, cloud security testing, and vulnerability assessments for enterprise environments. I have extensive experience analyzing complex attack surfaces, simulating adversarial techniques, and helping organizations strengthen their security posture against modern threats.

Through this platform I share technical research, security walkthroughs, vulnerability discoveries, testing methodologies, and practical insights from real-world engagements. The goal is to contribute to the security community by publishing high-quality technical content, responsible security research, and educational material for researchers, penetration testers, and security enthusiasts.
