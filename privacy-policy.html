Privacy Policy - OffSec Tool Box

Last updated: 22 August 2026

OffSec Tool Box ("the app") is an offline reference and calculator toolkit for security professionals and students. Your privacy matters, and the app is built to need none of your data.

Data we collect

None. OffSec Tool Box does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that gather data.

How the app works

All features - encoding/decoding, command references, the subnet calculator, the CVSS calculators, and the password generator - run entirely on your device and work offline. Anything you type (such as a target host or a value to encode) is processed locally and is never sent anywhere.

Screenshot OCR

The screenshot decode tool uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to read text from an image you choose. Images and the text extracted from them are processed on your device only and are not uploaded or retained by us.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (Pro and Pro+) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children's privacy

The app does not target children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
