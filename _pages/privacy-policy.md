You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads) or [www.aboutads.info](http://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

For more information on how Google uses data, visit: [How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 4. Consent Management (EEA, UK & Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we use a **Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP)** to obtain your consent before serving personalised advertisements or analytics cookies.

You have the following choices:

- **Consent** — allow personalised ads and analytics
- **Do not consent** — non-personalised ads only, no tracking cookies
- **Manage options** — granular control over individual consent categories

You may withdraw or change your consent at any time by clicking the **"Manage Consent"** link in the site footer.

We implement **Google Consent Mode v2**, which ensures:
- No ad personalisation data is collected without explicit consent
- Aggregated, cookieless measurement may still occur for service improvement
- Your choices are respected across all Google services used on this Site
